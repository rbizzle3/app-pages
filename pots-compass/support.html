Common Questions

What is POTS?

POTS (Postural Orthostatic Tachycardia Syndrome) is a form of dysautonomia characterized by an abnormal increase in heart rate when changing posture from lying down to standing. Common symptoms include lightheadedness, palpitations, fatigue, and brain fog. POTS Compass helps you track triggers and symptoms over time, but it is not a diagnostic tool.

How does the heart-rate integration work?

If you grant HealthKit access, POTS Compass reads heart-rate samples from Apple Health to correlate with your symptom logs. The app reads only; it never writes back to Health. You can revoke access in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → POTS Compass.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel via your App Store account: App Store → profile picture → Subscriptions → POTS Compass → Cancel. The developer can't cancel for you; Apple handles all billing.

How do I delete my data?

Open POTS Compass → Settings → "Delete All My Data". This clears everything stored on your device. Deleting the app from your Home screen also removes all data.
