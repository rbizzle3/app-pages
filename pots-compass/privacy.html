Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

POTS Compass is built on a simple principle: your data is yours, and the less of it we touch the better. This page explains exactly what data the app handles and what we (the developer) can see.

Data We Collect

None. POTS Compass does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information about you. There are no user accounts, no logins, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers.

Data Processed Locally on Your Device

The app processes the following on your device only (it never leaves the phone):

  • Symptom logs, trigger entries, and timing records you enter.
  • Optional heart-rate readings imported from Apple Health (with your permission).
  • Optional reminder times you set in Settings.

This data stays inside the app's sandbox on your iPhone. We have no access to it. If you delete the app, the data is deleted with it.

Apple Health Integration

If you grant HealthKit access, POTS Compass reads heart-rate samples to help you correlate symptoms with autonomic patterns. The app does not write back to Health. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → POTS Compass.

AI Features

POTS Compass uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework (iOS 26+) to generate plain-English pattern summaries from your logs. All AI processing happens entirely on your device. Nothing about your logs, triggers, or heart-rate data is sent to any server.

Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

POTS Compass offers in-app purchases and subscriptions processed through Apple's App Store. We never see your payment information; Apple handles all billing.

To cancel, open the App Store on your iPhone → tap your profile → Subscriptions → POTS Compass.

Data Deletion

Open the app → Settings → "Delete All My Data" to clear all logs, history, preferences. Deleting the app also removes all stored data.

Children's Privacy

POTS Compass is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Health Disclaimer

POTS Compass is not a medical device and is not a substitute for professional medical care. It is a self-tracking tool to help you observe patterns. Always consult a qualified physician about symptoms, diagnosis, or treatment.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we'll change the "Last updated" date above and post the new version at this URL.
