Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Exposure Ladder is built on a simple principle: your data is yours, and the less of it we touch the better. This page explains exactly what data the app handles and what we (the developer) can see.

Data We Collect

None. Exposure Ladder does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information about you. There are no user accounts, no logins, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers.

Data Processed Locally on Your Device

The app processes the following on your device only (it never leaves the phone):

  • Exposure hierarchy items, SUDS ratings, and session history you log.
  • Optional reminder times you set in Settings.

This data stays inside the app's sandbox on your iPhone. We have no access to it. If you delete the app, the data is deleted with it.

AI Features

Exposure Ladder uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework (iOS 26+) to generate plain-English coaching cues and session reflections. All AI processing happens entirely on your device. Nothing about your sessions, ratings, or hierarchy is sent to any server.

Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

Exposure Ladder offers in-app purchases and subscriptions processed through Apple's App Store. We never see your payment information; Apple handles all billing, refunds, and renewals.

To cancel, open the App Store on your iPhone → tap your profile → Subscriptions → Exposure Ladder.

Data Deletion

Open the app → Settings → "Delete All My Data" to clear all logs, history, preferences. Deleting the app also removes all stored data.

Children's Privacy

Exposure Ladder is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Health Disclaimer

Exposure Ladder is not a medical device and is not a substitute for professional mental-health care. It is a self-tracking tool. If you are experiencing severe distress, contact a qualified clinician or your local crisis line.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we'll change the "Last updated" date above and post the new version at this URL.
