Common Questions

What is SUDS?

SUDS (Subjective Units of Distress Scale) is a 0–100 scale used in ERP-style exposure work to rate the intensity of distress in a moment or during a session. 0 = none, 100 = the worst imaginable. The app lets you log a start and end SUDS for each session to track habituation over time.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel via your App Store account: App Store → profile picture → Subscriptions → Exposure Ladder → Cancel. The developer can't cancel for you; Apple handles all billing.

How do I delete my data?

Open Exposure Ladder → Settings → "Delete All My Data". This clears everything stored on your device. Deleting the app from your Home screen also removes all data.

Is this a replacement for therapy?

No. Exposure Ladder is a self-tracking tool. If you are working with a clinician, the app can help you log between sessions. If you are not working with one and your distress is severe, please reach out to a licensed mental-health professional or a crisis line.
