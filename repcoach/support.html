Common Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel via your App Store account: open the App Store on your iPhone → tap your profile picture → Subscriptions → RepCoach → Cancel Subscription. The developer can't cancel for you — Apple handles all billing.

How do I delete my data?

Open RepCoach → Settings → "Delete All My Data". This clears everything stored on your device. Deleting the app from your Home screen also removes all associated data.

Why does the app need camera/photo access?

RepCoach processes video on your device to deliver its core feature. Video data never leaves your phone. The permission is required for the feature to work; revoking it in iOS Settings will disable that feature but won't affect anything else.

I found a bug.

Email us with the steps to reproduce and your iPhone model + iOS version. Bug reports are read by a human, not a bot.

I have a feature request.

Send it! We can't promise to build everything, but real user requests shape the next release.
