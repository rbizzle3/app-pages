Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

Liftric is built on a simple principle: your data is yours, and the less of it we touch the better. This page explains exactly what data the app handles and what we (the developer) can see.

Data We Collect

None. Liftric does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information about you. There are no user accounts, no logins, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers.

Data Processed Locally on Your Device

The app processes the following on your device only (it never leaves the phone):

  • No device permissions are requested.

This data stays inside the app's sandbox on your iPhone. We have no access to it. If you delete the app, the data is deleted with it.

AI Features

Liftric uses Google Gemini (Flash Lite, gemini-3.1-flash-lite) to generate plain-English coaching cues from structured analysis results. Only a short text summary of the detected outcomes is sent to Google's API; raw video, audio, and image data never leaves your device. Gemini calls are made only with your explicit consent, which you can revoke at any time in Settings.

You can use Liftric without granting AI consent. When AI is disabled or unavailable, the app falls back to local rules-based coaching cues stored inside the app — no degraded experience.

Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

Liftric offers in-app purchases and subscriptions processed through Apple's App Store. We never see your payment information; Apple handles all billing, refunds, and renewals. Your subscription status is verified via StoreKit 2 on your device.

To cancel a subscription, open the App Store on your iPhone → tap your profile → Subscriptions → Liftric. Apple, not the app, controls cancellation and refund policy.

Data Deletion

To delete all data Liftric has stored on your device, open the app → Settings → "Delete All My Data". This clears all logs, history, preferences, and revokes any granted permissions. You can also simply delete the app from your Home screen.

Children's Privacy

Liftric is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we'll change the "Last updated" date above and post the new version at this URL. Substantive changes will be flagged inside the app itself.
